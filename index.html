02 Services

One fleet.
Six jobs.

A daily rental and a 5am airport run are not the same job. Blackline covers both from the same fleet, on the same corridor — arrange whichever you need in a single conversation.

  • 01

    Daily car rental

    The vehicle is yours from pickup to return, with the day planned around you rather than the other way around.

  • 02

    Airport transfers

    Timed pickups and drop-offs with the luggage actually counted, and the passenger count actually checked.

  • 03

    Private chauffeur

    A driver for the full day, or for the four hours of it that matter. Your itinerary, someone else at the wheel.

  • 04

    Intercity transport

    Douala, Buea and Limbe, and the corridor between them, treated as one route rather than three separate trips.

  • 05

    Business & executive

    Discreet transport for meetings, client pickups and site visits, arranged around a schedule that is not flexible.

  • 06

    Group transport

    Twelve seats in one vehicle, so the team arrives together and nobody is left negotiating a second taxi.

03 Why Blackline

Nothing hidden
in the small print.

  1. Convenient booking through WhatsApp

    No accounts, no forms that go nowhere. One message, written out for you, and a human reply.

  2. Multiple vehicle categories

    Four classes in the catalogue — from a five-seat sedan to a twelve-seat group van.

  3. Flexible transport options

    Daily rental, transfer, chauffeur or intercity — the same fleet, arranged to the job.

  4. Coverage across the corridor

    Douala, Buea and Limbe, with the route between them treated as one service area.

  5. Clear vehicle information first

    Capacity, luggage, transmission and rate are published before you commit to anything.
